Hi, I'm Frank.

My peer-to-peer story started with Napster in the 90s. I've been writing software professionally since 2010: first payment modules for online shops at Mediaopt, then research prototypes at the University of Göttingen, and from 2017 to 2021 blockchain and P2P technologies at MaibornWolff. In 2020 I founded web3.berlin. Since then my team and I have built and run production systems for Web3 protocols, and more and more for companies that want AI on their own terms.

You work directly with the engineers who build your system, from the first architecture sketch to the night something breaks. We write the code, deploy it and keep it running.
