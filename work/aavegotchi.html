Subgraphs & indexing

Every item, parcel and auction in the game, queryable in real time.

In Aavegotchi every item, land parcel and auction lives on-chain. The dApp, the Baazaar marketplace and the analytics all need that data fast, consistent and always available, while the game keeps shipping new features.

How it works

  1. Polygongame contracts emit events
  2. Subgraphscore, Gotchiverse, Baazaar

  4. AppsdApp, Baazaar, analytics, data API

DappRadar listed Aavegotchi among the top gaming dapps on Polygon. Source →

What we built

  • The core, Gotchiverse and Baazaar subgraphs: lending, land parcels, installations, access rights, GBM auctions and buy orders.
  • A data API, including the circulating-supply endpoint used by market data sites.
  • An analytics site for the Gotchiverse economy.
  • A load balancer in front of self-hosted Graph nodes for development and production.
  • Unit tests and CI pipelines that deploy subgraphs automatically.
