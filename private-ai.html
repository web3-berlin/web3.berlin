Typical setups

We size the hardware to your team and use case.

In your office

GPU workstation

One machine with one to four GPUs on your premises. Good for a team that wants an assistant and document search, with the data never leaving the building.

In an EU data centre

Dedicated GPU server

A server rented only for you, for example at Hetzner in Finland. No hardware in your office, still no AI provider in between.

Scale

Small GPU cluster

Several GPU servers managed with Kubernetes, on-premise or in the data centre. For many users, larger models and automated workflows.

Questions

What clients usually ask first.

Does our data go to an AI provider?

No. The models, your documents and every prompt stay on your own server, whether that's in your office or a dedicated server in an EU data centre. Nothing is sent to OpenAI, Google or any other AI provider.

Which models can it run?

Open-weight models such as Qwen, DeepSeek or Liquid AI's LFM family, chosen for your use case and your hardware. We switch or add models as better ones come out.

What does it cost?

That depends on the hardware and what you want to do with it. Tell us your use case and we'll send you a rough estimate for hardware, setup and maintenance.

Who looks after it once it's running?

We can, with updates, monitoring and model upgrades. Or we hand it over to your IT team with full documentation.

Does this help with data protection?

Because nothing is sent to an outside AI provider, there's no data transfer to document for that part. We help you describe the setup for your data protection officer.
